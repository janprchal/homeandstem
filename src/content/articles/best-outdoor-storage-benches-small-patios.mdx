A small patio doesn't leave room for a shed, but cushions and garden tools still need somewhere dry to live. An outdoor storage bench solves both problems in one footprint: hidden storage underneath and extra seating on top. We compared resin and rattan options built for tight spaces and picked four worth buying.

## What to Look for in an Outdoor Storage Bench

### Material and weather resistance

Most outdoor storage benches are molded from UV-treated resin or PE rattan wrapped around a steel frame. Resin holds up best in direct sun and won't rust, while rattan-over-metal looks more like real furniture but needs a tight weave to keep rain out. Check the material before buying if your patio gets full sun or heavy rain, since a cheap resin blend can fade or crack within a season.

### Storage capacity vs. actual footprint

Gallon capacity can be misleading on a small patio. A 70-gallon bench and a 40-gallon bench can take up a similar amount of floor space if one is taller and narrower and the other is wider and shorter. The gallon count won't tell you if it fits. Check the actual dimensions against your available space before ordering.

### Seating capacity and weight rating

If the bench doubles as seating, check the weight rating. The word "bench" in a listing doesn't guarantee it holds two adults. Anything under 300 lbs works as a lid to perch on for a minute. It won't hold up to regular use by two people.

### Assembly and lockability

Nearly every bench in this category ships flat and needs tool-free panel assembly, usually 20 to 50 minutes for two people. If you're storing anything you'd rather kids or neighbors not get into, look for a lid with a lock-ready latch. A padlock is rarely included.
