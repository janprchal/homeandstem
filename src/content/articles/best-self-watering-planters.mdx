## What to Look for in a Self-Watering Planter

### Reservoir size

Bigger reservoirs mean longer gaps between watering, which matters most on an exposed
balcony.

### Water-level indicator

A visible gauge takes the guesswork out of refills and helps you avoid overwatering.

### Material and UV resistance

Balcony planters sit in direct sun for most of the day. Look for UV-stable resin that won't
fade or crack within a season.

<CalloutBox variant="tip" title="Testing tip">
  Fill the reservoir, then check soil moisture after 7 days before topping up. Most planters
  outlast your instinct to water.
</CalloutBox>
