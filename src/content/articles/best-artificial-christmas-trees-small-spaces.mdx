## How to Choose a Christmas Tree for a Small Space

### Check width first, then height

A tree's footprint decides whether it fits, so compare widths first. In this guide, 21.75 inches (HOMCOM) and 27.5 to 28 inches (Best Choice Products and National Tree) are slim. The 4.5-foot Puleo is 37 inches wide.

### Leave room for a topper

A standard ceiling is about 8 feet. A 6 to 6.5 foot tree leaves space for a star or bow. Measure your own ceiling, especially if you have a light fixture or a slanted wall.

### Keep it away from heat

Fire safety experts advise keeping a tree at least 3 feet from any heat source, such as a fireplace, radiator, or heating vent. In a small room that can decide where the tree goes, so check the spot before you buy.

### Pre-lit saves time, but plan for the outlet

All four trees come pre-lit, which saves hours of untangling. You still need an outlet within reach, and some trees use a plug that connects the light strings from section to section. Check that the cord reaches your corner.

### Plan the storage

Pencil trees fold down narrower than full trees, but they still need a home for 11 months. A tree storage bag helps. Our [holiday storage guide](/best-storage-solutions-holiday-decorations) has options for trees, lights, and ornaments.
