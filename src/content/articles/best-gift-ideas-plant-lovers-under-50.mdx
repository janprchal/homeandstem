## How to Choose a Gift for a Plant Lover

### Buy tools, not plants

Plants are personal, and a friend may already own the one you pick. Tools and accessories are always useful, and every item above is $20 or less.

### Match the gift to the habit

If your friend roots cuttings, buy the propagation station. If they bring home every succulent they see, buy the terrarium kit. If their plants sit in a dark corner, buy the grow light.

### Pair a small gift with a plant

A $10 mister and a small pothos make a good $25 gift. Swap in the watering can and you reach about $45.

### Check the budget

All six picks come in under $50 each. Combine two and you land between $25 and $40.
