The EGO POWER+ LB5302 is a genuine gas-blower replacement with real power behind it. The Greenworks 40V is a lighter, cheaper sweeper built for quick patio and driveway cleanup rather than deep leaf piles. We compare both to find which one fits a small yard best.

## Power and performance

CFM (cubic feet per minute) is the number that matters most for moving leaves, and the LB5302's 530 CFM is nearly four times the Greenworks' 135 CFM. In practice, that means the EGO clears a thick pile of wet, matted leaves in one or two passes, while the Greenworks needs several passes and struggles once leaves get damp or compacted. The Greenworks does win on raw air speed (150 MPH vs. 110 MPH), which helps it move light, dry debris off a hard surface fast. Speed without volume is why it feels underpowered on a real leaf pile.

## Weight and handling

At 3.27 lbs, the Greenworks is one-handed and easy to manage, good for quick jobs or anyone who doesn't want to wrestle with a bigger tool. The EGO is heavier and works best held with both hands during turbo bursts, though its balanced design keeps it from feeling top-heavy during normal use. For a small yard used a few times a week, the Greenworks' lighter weight is a real advantage. For anyone doing a full fall cleanup across a bigger lot, the EGO's extra heft comes with proportionally more work done per minute.

## Price and value

$149.00 for the EGO against $79.99 for the Greenworks is a real gap, and that's before factoring in that EGO batteries work across their entire 56V tool lineup, including trimmers, hedge trimmers, and even a chainsaw, while Greenworks batteries only cross over within their own 40V line. If a leaf blower is the only cordless yard tool on your list, the Greenworks is the better dollar-for-dollar buy for small-yard cleanup. If you're building out a battery platform or you have real acreage to clear, the EGO's extra cost buys capability you'll use.
