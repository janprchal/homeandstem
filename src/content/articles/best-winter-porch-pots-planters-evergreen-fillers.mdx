## How to Build a Winter Porch Pot

### Start with the right container

Choose a pot that is made for the outdoors and has a drainage hole. A pot without drainage collects meltwater and can crack when it freezes.

### Add height first

Push an urn filler or a few evergreen branches into the center. Height makes the pot look full from the street. Fillers in these listings are 24 to 24.5 inches tall.

### Light it with batteries

Battery lights save you from running cords across a porch. Set the timer so the lights come on at dusk and go off on their own. Keep spare batteries in a drawer, because cold shortens battery life.

### Lift the pot off the floor

Risers keep the pot from freezing to the porch and let water drain. They cost a few dollars and protect the floor.

### Plan for January

If you want the display to last past the holidays, skip the red berries and keep to plain greens. A winter porch pot can look good until early spring.
