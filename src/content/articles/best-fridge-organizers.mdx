## What to Look for in Fridge Organizers

### Measure first

Measure the shelf depth, width, and the height between shelves. A bin that is too tall blocks the shelf above it, and a turntable needs more room than its own size because it spins. Check the manufacturer's listing for the minimum rotation space.

### Clear, solid sides

Clear bins let you see what you have. Solid sides catch spills. For raw meat, UMN Extension says to store it on the bottom shelf so juices do not drip onto ready-to-eat food, and Penn State Extension adds trays or bags to contain juices.

### Do not overfill

UMN and Penn State Extension both say not to overload the refrigerator. UMN says to leave space for air to circulate. Bins should group food, not pack the shelf.

### Check the temperature

The USDA says a fridge should stay at 40°F or below and that an appliance thermometer is the way to check it. After you reorganize, put a thermometer on a middle shelf.
