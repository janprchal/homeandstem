Clearance swings by inches between bed frames: a rigid plastic bin that jams under one frame slides right under the next, and a bag stuffed with sweaters turns into dead weight the moment you have to drag it out for last season's boots. These five containers cover the real range: a wheeled bin you can pull out one-handed, a low-profile box for frames with barely six inches to spare, and a fabric option cheap enough to buy in bulk.

## What to Look for in an Under-Bed Storage Container

### Measure your actual clearance first

Bed frame height varies from around 4 inches on a platform bed to well over a foot on a traditional frame with a box spring. Measure before buying: a rigid bin with a raised lid can be an inch or two taller than it looks in photos, and that inch is the difference between it sliding in smoothly and not fitting at all.

### Wheels versus a flat bottom

Wheels make a heavy, fully loaded bin far easier to pull out from under a low bed, but they also add height, which can be a problem on a tight-clearance frame. A flat-bottomed bin sits lower but means more crouching and pulling by hand, especially once it's full.

### Clear, tinted, or fabric

Clear plastic makes finding things fast but shows dust and clutter inside. Tinted or grey plastic hides mess better while still being visible enough to identify contents. Fabric bags fold flat for storage but offer no protection from moisture, so they're best for clothing and bedding rather than anything that needs to stay dry or pest-free.

### How often you'll open it

A latching lid or a full zip closure is worth the extra few dollars for anything you're storing long-term, like out-of-season decor. For things you're pulling out every week or two, prioritize easy access over a tight seal.
