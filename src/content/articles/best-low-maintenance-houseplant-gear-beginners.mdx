## What to Look for in Houseplant Tools

### Start with the problem you have

Yellow leaves and mushy stems point to overwatering, so buy the moisture meter first. Tiny flies mean fungus gnats. A plant that has outgrown its pot needs repotting tools. Buy for the problem you have, not the full set.

### Keep tools small and cheap

Houseplant pots are small, so full-size garden tools get in the way. A mini set and a 40-ounce can handle most apartments.

### Clean tools between plants

Wipe the meter probe and the snips with a damp cloth or rubbing alcohol when you move from one plant to the next. It keeps pests and rot from spreading.
