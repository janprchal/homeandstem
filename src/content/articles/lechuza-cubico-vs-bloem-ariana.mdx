## Reservoir and watering interval

The Cubico's sealed reservoir and gauge tell you exactly when to refill, typically every
two to three weeks. The Ariana's wicking disk pulls water up from a shallow base tray as
the soil dries; it needs checking more often, especially outdoors in hot weather, since
there's no gauge to tell you the level.

## Price and value

The gap here is stark: the Cubico runs $111.75 against the Ariana's $12.29, over nine
times the price. If you're outfitting a whole balcony, the Ariana's lower price adds up
fast in your favor. For one or two statement plants where reliable watering matters more
than upfront cost, the Cubico earns its premium.
