Indoor plants that stretch, pale, or stall every winter need more light. A good grow light
fixes that, but the specs can be confusing. This guide covers what matters, and the light
we'd buy.

## What to Look for in a Grow Light

### Spectrum

Full-spectrum (white) LEDs cover the wavelengths most houseplants need and look pleasant in
a living room, unlike the old purple "blurple" panels.

### PPFD and coverage

Photosynthetic photon flux density tells you how much usable light reaches the leaves.
Match the fixture's coverage area to your plant grouping.

### Mounting and timer

Look for adjustable mounting and a built-in timer so plants get a consistent 12–16 hour
day without you remembering to flip a switch.
