## How to Choose a Holiday Plant Gift

### Think about pets first

Per the ASPCA, the Christmas cactus is non-toxic to cats and dogs. Amaryllis, paperwhite narcissus, and Norfolk Island pine are toxic if chewed. If the recipient has a curious cat, the cactus is the safe choice.

### Match the plant to the light

Christmas cactus and Norfolk pine like bright, indirect light. Amaryllis and paperwhites need a sunny window only while the stalks grow. A friend with a dark apartment will do better with bulbs than with a pine.

### Pick a bloom time

For gifts on Christmas Day, start bulbs in late November. Paperwhites take three to five weeks and amaryllis four to six, so order by mid-November if you want flowers on the day.

### Plan for shipping

Live plants travel badly in cold weather. Check the forecast for your recipient, and have it delivered to a home where someone will be there to bring it inside.
