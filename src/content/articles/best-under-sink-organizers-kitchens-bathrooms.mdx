## What to Look for in an Under-Sink Organizer

### Measure the pipes and the opening

Every sink cabinet is different. Measure the width and depth of the opening, the height under the pipes, and where the drain and supply lines run. A U-shaped or L-shaped unit goes around the pipe. A solid shelf does not.

### Height

Tall bottles are the problem. Check the listing for the clear height of each tier. The DEKAVA's bottom area takes items up to 10.3 inches. The L-shaped pick adjusts to 16.9 inches.

### Material and moisture

Under a sink, leaks happen. ABS and acrylic do not rust, and a metal frame with a coating holds up better than bare steel. Wipe up spills and check the cabinet every few months for leaks.

### Pull-out or fixed

A pull-out tier lets you reach the back without emptying the cabinet. A door mount or a bag dispenser frees the floor for tall items.
