## What to Look for in Holiday Decoration Storage

### Match the container to the decoration

No single container works for everything. Fragile ornaments need individual compartments, lights need something to wrap around, and a tree needs a bag long enough for its sections. One big tote for all of it means broken ornaments in December.

### Soft bag or rigid tote

Fabric boxes and bags are lighter, cost less, and fold flat when empty. Rigid plastic totes cost more but resist crushing, which matters if you stack boxes or store in a garage. If everything sits on a closet shelf with nothing on top, soft storage works well.

### Check the size against your storage spot

Measure the closet shelf, the under-bed gap, or the garage corner before you order. Under-bed containers run low and long, and tree bags run tall and wide. A container that doesn't fit its spot ends up on the closet floor.

### Label and keep like with like

A clear panel, a label window, or a clear tote lets you find the right box in a dark closet next December. Keep one container per decoration type, and write the contents on the outside before you close it.
