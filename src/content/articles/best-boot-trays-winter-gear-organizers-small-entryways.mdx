## What to Look for in Boot Trays and Winter Gear Storage

### Tray size

Measure the spot first, then count the pairs you need to park. The trays here run from 27.5 inches (SUPENUIN) to 32 inches (Gorilla Grip) long, so pick the one that fits your door area.

### Edge height

Look for a raised edge. The Gorilla Grip's 0.8-inch edge, for example, is rated to hold up to 20 cups of liquid. A flat mat has no such reserve.

### Material

Both trays here are rubber and have a textured surface, which gives wet soles something to grip. Whatever you choose, make sure it has a lip.

### Wall space and height

In a narrow entry, go vertical. Wall hooks and tall racks use the wall instead of the floor, but check shelf spacing against your tallest boots before you order.
